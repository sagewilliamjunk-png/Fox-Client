To further strengthen the plan, here are a few recommended additions and structural refinements based on common Minecraft client pitfalls:

1. Recommended Additions
Step 2C: Resource Pack & Shader Compatibility (NEW)

Why: Since you are using Sodium, users will expect Iris Shaders support. Adding Iris as a dependency early ensures your custom rendering (Starry Sky, Chunk Borders) doesn't break when shaders are enabled.

Files: build.gradle.

Step 3F: Keybind Manager (NEW)

Why: With ToggleSprint, Free Look, and the HUD Editor all needing keys, you need a central way to prevent "keybind overlap" where one key triggers three different things.

Files: util/KeybindManager.java.

Step 4E: UI Scaling & Resolution Fixes (NEW)


Why: Drag-and-drop HUDs often "break" when a user changes their GUI Scale from 2 to 3. Adding a "Relative Positioning" check (storing coordinates as percentages of screen width/height) is critical during the stabilization phase.

2. Strategic Refinements
Move Optimization Deps (Step 6) Earlier:

Change: Move Step 6 (Performance Mods) to occur immediately after Step 2.

Reasoning: Testing "Chunk Borders" (5E) or "Starry Sky" (2A) on a vanilla engine vs. Sodium can produce different rendering bugs. It is safer to build your custom HUD and Render modules on top of the optimized engine they will actually be used with.

Clarification on "Free Look" (3C):

Note: Ensure this includes a "Self-Auto-Reset" feature. If a player toggles Free Look and forgets, it can be disorienting. Most clients reset the camera to the forward position once the key is released.

3. Updated "Gray-Zone" Guidance
Regarding the modules you asked about in Source 5:


Reach Display: Safe. High value for PvP, strictly visual.

Hitbox Tweaks: DANGEROUS. If this modifies the size of the hitbox, it is a "cheat" and will get users banned. Keep it strictly as a Hitbox Renderer (visual only).

Weather/Time Changer: Safe/Standard. A common client-side aesthetic feature.

Anti AFK: RISKY. Many servers (Hypixel, etc.) detect the rhythmic movement of Anti-AFK scripts and ban for it. If included, add a "Use at your own risk" warning in the UI.
